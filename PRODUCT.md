# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

UK shoppers aged 30–45 who buy fewer garments and keep them. They want a proper shop: sizes, shipping, returns, care — not a hype drop page.

## Product Purpose

FENT sells a short clothing list cut from mill cloth. The site must look like its own house, not a copy of a live streetwear catalog, and it must carry the information a clothing brand is expected to publish.

## Brand Personality

Exact, dry, unhurried. A red seal on cotton paper. No countdown clocks, no gender as the first click, no cloned model carousels.

## Anti-references

- The Souled Store (and any clone of its header, hero carousel, pill chips, or product-card bars)
- ASOS / Boohoo sale banners and Women|Men as the homepage
- COS oatmeal loft
- Joules countryside navy
- Editorial italic-serif magazine fashion AI

## Design Principles

- Own composition: still-life cloth, not studio model grids.
- House colours stay red and off-white; layout does not stay catalog-template.
- Publish the boring pages: size, ship, return, care, legal, contact.
- One wardrobe. Departments are cloth, not gender.
- Fewer pieces, named like stock, priced in GBP.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Body ≥4.5:1 (target 7:1). Skip link, visible focus, reduced motion. Do not require a gender choice to enter the shop.
